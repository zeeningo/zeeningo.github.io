Meet Your Teacher

Rooted in West African strength, moved by breath

Zee Ateh Ningo is a certified yoga instructor (E-RYT 200) with over 2,000 teaching hours, specializing in strength-based, dynamic flows that bridge yoga, lifting, and functional movement. Oya Movement is a practice of transformation — drawing on the fierce, feminine power of change, with ancestry from Mankon and Pinyin in Cameroon, weaving heritage, joy, and intention into every session.

Whether we're flowing through vinyasa or building strength, the invitation is the same: come home to your body, and let it carry you somewhere new. Zee has led teacher trainings and mentorship programs, and is known for integrating inversion and contortion work to help students explore new possibilities in their practice.

  • E-RYT 200 · 2,000+ teaching hours
  • 300-Hour Yoga Teacher Certification (in progress)
  • Schwinn Indoor Cycling Instructor Power Certification
  • M.P.H., University of California, Irvine
